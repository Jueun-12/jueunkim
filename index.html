Jueun (June) Kim

PhD Candidate in Strategic Management

Schulich School of Business, York University

“Thank you for visiting my website.

I hope you enjoy getting to know a little more about me, my research, and the questions that keep me curious. I would also love to stay connected through LinkedIn.

To me, strategy and entrepreneurship are more than subjects we study in business school. They are ways of thinking about life: how we use what we are given, choose where to spend our limited time and attention, and move toward something we cannot fully see. In that sense, we are all strategists and entrepreneurs in our own lives.

That way of thinking drew me from practice into research, and it continues to shape how I teach. What keeps me curious is a smaller puzzle within it. The same act rarely means the same thing twice. Its meaning depends on who acts, who is watching, and what they already believe about the person acting. I have been chasing that question ever since, in my research and, honestly, everywhere else.”
